Tasks serve as a tool for collaborating on time-bound, measurable initiatives. As a member, you will interact with tasks through your personal profile and projects. Members can be assigned tasks by the managers of the projects they are a part of. 

## Receiving Task Assignments

When a task is assigned to you, you will receive an automated email from [donotreply@openfuturecoalition.org](mailto:donotreply@openfuturecoalition.org) informing you of the new assignment.

## Viewing Your Tasks

Once assigned, you can access and view your tasks in two main areas:

- **Personal Feed:** All tasks assigned to you will appear in the Feed of your profile
- **Project Feed:** Within a specific project's Feed of which you are a member, you will see your tasks associated with that project 
- **Calendar:** Any task with a set start and end date will appear on your personal profile calendar and the project’s shared calendar. (To view tasks on the calendar, click the gear icon to change the default.) 

## Completing a Task

To work on and finalize an assigned task: 

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Task">
    From your Feed's task list, click the arrow on the right side of the task row to open the full task details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Contributions">
    You can record several types of data depending on the task requirements:

    - Time Tracking: Note the time contributed to the task. You can add multiple rows. 
    - Reporting Data: For "Measurable Result" tasks, enter specific quantities, dates, and descriptions related to project goals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or Finalize">
    - Select "Save" to keep your progress and return to the task later.
    - Select "Mark Complete" once you have finished the work. 
  </Step>
</Steps>

Approval Process: Once marked complete, the task may be sent to the Project Managers or the Organization for review and final approval.

# FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I view and respond to an assigned task?">
    Log into your personal profile, find the "Tasks" list on your Feed tab, and click the arrow on the right side of the task row to open the task, enter data, add attachments, and mark it complete when you are ready.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who can assign or manage tasks on behalf of a project?">
    Task management is performed by the Project Creator (Admin) and designated Project Managers.
  </Accordion>
